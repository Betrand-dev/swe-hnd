# English / Bilingual Training Paper Overview

## Objective
The exam evaluates the candidate's professional communication capabilities to:
* Interpret, analyze, and synthesize complex technical and engineering documents in English.
* Demonstrate functional written, oral, and operational corporate communication skills within a professional tech team.
* Produce precise technical reports, project documentation, and summaries regarding software development cycles.
* Command specialized English vocabulary relevant to the computing, IT, and software industries.

---

## Paper Content
* **Paper Name:** English (Bilingual Training / Language Skills)
* **Nature of Exam:** Written
* **Duration:** 2 Hours
* **Credit Value:** 2 *(Typically mirrors fundamental generic skills categories)*
* **Structure:** The written paper evaluates standard grammar mechanics, professional composition, and technical vocabulary translation within engineering scenarios.

---

## What You Should Know
To successfully clear the English evaluation as a Software Engineer, you must know:
* **Technical Software Vocabulary:** Command terminology around algorithm parameters, network routing, database management systems (DBMS), data normalization, and system design structures.
* **Corporate Communication:** Standard formats for software deployment documentation, client proposal editing, user instruction manuals, and team project update structures.
* **Bilingual Translation & Analysis:** Ability to securely read, translate, and synthesize system constraints or logic specifications back and forth between English and French.
* **Professional Grammar & Writing:** Proper structural mechanics for writing business correspondence, project bug reports, testing reviews, and computing surveys.

---

## Marks Allocation

The language assessment is graded out of a total of 100 marks (100%), generally structured as follows:


| Section | Evaluation Format & Syllabus Weights | Recommended Time | Marks Allocated | Percentage Weight |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Section A** | **Technical Vocabulary & Reading Comprehension:** Text parsing, interpreting technical case requirements, and specialized IT term match-ups. | 45 Minutes | 30 Marks | 30% |
| **Section B** | **Grammar and Structural Mechanics:** Error corrections, sentence synthesis, and logical syntax assembly. | 35 Minutes | 30 Marks | 30% |
| **Section C** | **Professional Writing and Technical Essay:** Constructing structured technical reports, email configurations to team leads, or summarizing a software incident analysis. | 40 Minutes | 40 Marks | 40% |
| **TOTAL** | **Complete Written Paper** | **02 Hours** | **100 Marks** | **100%** |
