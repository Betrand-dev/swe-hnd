# Discrete Mathematics Paper Overview

## Objective
The exam evaluates the candidate's fundamental computing logic capabilities to:
* Comprehend and manipulate core logical elements of discrete mathematics.
* Understand and apply logical concepts related to mathematical analysis.
* Parse, represent, and analyze statistical structures.
* Solve data uncertainty problems deploying probability formulas.

---

## Paper Content
* **Paper Name:** Discrete Mathematics
* **Code:** SWE18
* **Nature of Exam:** Written
* **Duration:** 4 Hours
* **Credit Value:** 4
* **Structure:** The written evaluation is divided into 2 distinct functional parts: Section A utilizes Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), and Section B utilizes open-ended, structured evaluation fields.

---

## What You Should Know
To successfully resolve the mathematical sets, equations, and structures in this paper, you must know:
* **Discrete Concepts:** Propositional logic, matrices, proofs, set structures, and structural computer graph algorithms.
* **Mathematical Analysis:** Sequential numeric limits, series behaviors, differential structures, and functional computation routines.
* **Statistics:** Grouped data formatting, calculating central tendencies (mean, median, variance), dispersion models, and chart representation schemas.
* **Probability:** Independent and dependent events, counting formulations (permutations, combinations), probability distribution states, and conditional probability calculations.
