# Digital Electronics Paper Overview

## Objective
The exam evaluates the candidate's core competencies to:
* Master binary, octal, and hexadecimal number systems.
* Perform structural conversions from one number system to another.
* Understand the core concepts of computer fundamentals.
* Demonstrate proficiency in key office applications (Word, Excel, or PowerPoint).
* Understand the concept of living online and internet communication frameworks.

---

## Paper Content
* **Paper Name:** Digital Electronics
* **Code:** SWE19
* **Nature of Exam:** Written
* **Duration:** 4 Hours
* **Credit Value:** 7
* **Structure:** The written evaluation is divided into two distinct parts: Section A consists of Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs), and Section B consists of structural/open-ended questions.

---

## What You Should Know
To successfully clear this theoretical and fundamental paper, you must know:
* **Number Systems & Codes:** Arithmetic operations and structural conversion formulas between binary, octal, decimal, and hexadecimal formats.
* **Computer Hardware:** Fundamental building blocks of a computer, internal architecture, and component interfaces.
* **Networks & Mobile Devices:** Fundamental concepts of connectivity, wireless and wired interactions, and structural mobile system mechanisms.
* **Application Software:** High-level functionality and structural usage of production suites, specifically Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.
* **Living Online:** Web architecture, data transmission pipelines, and structural internet communication profiles.

---
