# Law and Citizenship Education Paper Overview

## Objective
The exam evaluates the candidate's cross-cutting knowledge of legal and civic structures to:
* Understand the core sources of law and fundamental human rights within the Cameroonian legal system.
* Analyze the civic responsibilities, ethical behaviors, and moral reasoning required of a citizen.
* Comprehend workplace rights and the legal dynamics regulating employment contracts.
* Apply standard commercial laws governing business transactions, liability, and intellectual property.
* Navigate the foundational legal requirements for company formation, governance, and dissolution.

---

## Paper Content
* **Paper Name:** Law and Citizenship Education
* **Code:** LCE10
* **Nature of Exam:** Written
* **Duration:** 2 Hours
* **Credit Value:** 2
* **Structure:** A comprehensive transversal examination mandatory for all specialties. The paper is organized into 5 distinct thematic modules: Fundamental Law, Civics & Ethics, Labour Law, Business Law, and Company Law.

---

## What You Should Know
To successfully clear this legal evaluation framework, you must master:
* **Law & Fundamental Rights:** Sources of law in Cameroon, classification of crimes, fundamental human rights, and the role of oversight bodies like the National Human Rights Commission.
* **Civics, Ethics & Morals:** Rights and duties of a citizen, methods of obtaining or losing Cameroonian citizenship, arms and types of government, and Kohlberg's stages of ethical reasoning.
* **Labour Law (*Droit du Travail*):** Definitions of a worker, types of employment contracts (specified vs. unspecified duration), the legal parameters and durations of probation periods, obligations of employers and employees, and conditions surrounding wrongful termination under the Cameroonian Labour Code.
* **Business Law:** Financial and transactional regulations, specific banker-customer duties (such as confidentiality), debtor/creditor relationships, mortgages, and taxation principles in Cameroon.
* **Company Law:** Rules governing business entities, formation criteria, operational compliance, and legal reasons leading to the dissolution of a company under the OHADA Uniform Act.
