# French / Bilingual Training Paper Overview

## Objective
The exam evaluates the candidate's professional communication capabilities in French to:
* Read, analyze, and extract technical details from corporate and engineering documents written in French.
* Establish fluid written and operational corporate communication inside professional tech spaces.
* Compose clear corporate documents such as professional emails, formal notifications, or cover letters (*Lettres de motivation*) targeting directors or tech leads.
* Master fundamental grammar rules, structural mechanics, and precise conjugation structures expected in a professional francophone or bilingual workplace.

---

## Paper Content
* **Paper Name:** French (Bilingual Training / Language Skills)
* **Nature of Exam:** Written
* **Duration:** 2 Hours
* **Credit Value:** 2 *(Standard allocation for mandatory cross-cutting language requirements)*
* **Structure:** The paper features an integrated assessment split between structural Multiple-Choice Questions (MCQs), targeted grammar/conjugation problems, text reading comprehension, and professional technical essay composition.

---

## What You Should Know
To secure high marks in the French national assessment component, you must be able to master:
* **Grammar & Verb Conjugation (*Grammaire et Conjugaison*):** Precise command over past, present, and future indicative timelines (*Passé simple, Imparfait, Passé composé, Futur antérieur*) alongside conditional and imperative modes. You must also master the structural rules of past participle agreement (*Accord du participe passé*).
* **Reading Comprehension & Analysis (*Compréhension de texte*):** Breaking down general or technology-focused text blocks, isolating situational constraints, and providing structural syntheses of ideas.
* **Professional Composition (*Expression Écrite*):** Crafting structured business correspondence using appropriate opening and closing protocols, applying professional vocabulary templates, and formatting technical incident reports or standard job applications.
* **Translation Principles (*Traduction*):** Translating simple software, layout design, or data criteria parameters cleanly from English technical definitions into functional French expressions.

---
