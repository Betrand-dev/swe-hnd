# Digital Literacy Paper Overview

## Objective
The exam evaluates the candidate's core competencies to:
* Define digital literacy and its core concepts.
* Understand the role of computers and technology in modern society.
* Comprehend the processes involved in digitizing physical information.
* Detail the historical evolution of computing and computer utilization.
* Apply operational web knowledge and navigate a digital society.

---

## Paper Content
* **Paper Name:** Digital Literacy
* **Code:** SWE11
* **Nature of Exam:** Written
* **Duration:** 3 Hours * (Note: Header specifies 2H, but total summary structure specifies 3H)*
* **Credit Value:** 2
* **Structure:** The written paper is divided into 2 primary sections: Section A covers general computer knowledge (split into MCQs and structural questions), and Section B focuses on web knowledge and the digital society.

---

## What You Should Know
To successfully answer the questions in this paper, you must know:
* **General Computer Knowledge:** Definition of digital literacy, historical landmarks in computer science, how systems process and digitize data, and the sociopolitical impacts of computing devices.
* **Web Knowledge:** Core internet mechanisms, search engine operations, Web 2.0 paradigms, and social media platforms.
* **Digital Society Operations:** Cyber crime prevention, software piracy regulations, online monetization methods, e-commerce architectures, e-learning platform integrations, cloud storage systems, and the Internet of Things (IoT).
